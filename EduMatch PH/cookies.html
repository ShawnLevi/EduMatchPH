Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Development note: this build uses your browser's local storage (not traditional server-set cookies) to keep the demo self-contained. The table below lists exactly what's stored, under the technical name it actually uses, so this policy stays accurate to how the site really works.

This policy explains what EduMatch PH stores in your browser, why, and how you can control it.

1. What we mean by "cookies" here

Cookies and local storage both let a website remember information on your device between visits — for example, staying logged in without re-entering your password on every page. We use the term "cookies" throughout this policy in the everyday sense, covering both.

2. What we store

Storage key Category Purpose Duration
edumatch_current_user Essential Keeps you logged in as you move between pages. Until you log out
edumatch_remember_username Preference Pre-fills your username if you checked "Remember me" at login. Until cleared
edumatch_cookie_consent Essential Remembers your choice on this cookie banner, so it doesn't show again. Until cleared
edumatch_demo_users Essential Stores registered demo accounts so login/registration work without a live server. Until cleared
edumatch_profile_<username> Essential Stores your saved scholarship profile details (school, course, income range, etc.). Until cleared

3. Your choices

The banner shown on the login page lets you accept or decline non-essential storage the first time you visit. Essential items — the ones that make login and your saved profile work at all — can't be disabled without breaking core features, in the same way a website can't keep you logged in without storing something to prove who you are.

You can clear everything at any time through your browser:

  • Chrome / Edge: Settings → Privacy and security → Clear browsing data → Cookies and site data.
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Clear Data.
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data → Remove.

Clearing this data will log you out and remove your saved demo profile.

4. Third-party cookies

We don't use third-party advertising or tracking cookies. The only external resource this site loads is Google Fonts, which may set its own cookies under Google's policies when the font files are fetched.

5. Changes to this policy

If what we store changes — for example, once this moves from a local demo to a real backend with server-side sessions — this page will be updated to match, and the "Last updated" date above will change.
