EduMatch PH ("we", "us") helps students find scholarships and school programs that match their profile. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: full name, username, email address, and password (stored as a hash, never in plain text).
  • Profile information: academic details you provide, such as course, year level, GPA, income range, and location, used to match you with scholarships.
  • Usage information: pages you visit and actions you take within the site, used to improve the matching experience.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To generate scholarship and school recommendations based on your profile.
  • To communicate with you about your account, such as password resets.
  • To improve the site and fix issues.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Scholarship providers or schools, only when you choose to apply or express interest through the platform.
  • Service providers who help us run the platform (e.g. hosting), bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities, if required by law.

4. Data retention

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

5. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the right to be informed, to access your data, to correct it, to object to certain processing, and to request erasure, subject to legal or contractual restrictions.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical measures — such as password hashing — to protect your information. No system is 100% secure, and we encourage you to use a strong, unique password.

7. Children's privacy

EduMatch PH is intended for college-level students. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be noted on this page with an updated date.
