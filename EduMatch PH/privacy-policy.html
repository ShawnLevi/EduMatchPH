EduMatch PH ("we", "us") helps scholarship administrators review and rank student applications for scholarships and aid programs. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it is handled in the admin workflow.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: administrator name, email address, and password used to access the scholarship review dashboard.
  • Application review data: applicant details used for screening, ranking, comparison, and shortlist decisions, such as GPA, course, household income, location, and eligibility status.
  • Usage information: pages visited and actions taken within the admin system, used to improve internal review workflows and detect operational issues.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage administrator access to the dashboard.
  • To screen applicants against scholarship criteria.
  • To keep application records, rankings, and shortlist decisions organized for review.
  • To improve the site, audit internal decisions, and fix issues.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We may share it with:

  • Scholarship providers or review committees, only when the administrator exports or forwards records for official evaluation.
  • Service providers who help us run the platform, bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities, if required by law.

4. Data retention

We keep administrator and application-review information for as long as the scholarship cycle or program requires. Records may be archived or removed according to internal review policies.

5. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, administrators and applicants have a right to be informed, to access data, to correct inaccurate details, to object to some processing, and to request erasure when legally applicable.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to secure admin access and application data. No system is 100% secure, so strong passwords and access controls remain essential.

7. Sensitive information

EduMatch PH may process educational, financial, and personal information to support scholarship screening. We do not knowingly process data for individuals under the age limit set by applicable law or institutional policy without proper authorization.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be noted on this page with an updated date.
