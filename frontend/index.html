Two-stage scholarship workflow

Designed so every eligibility check is auditable and administrators always make the final decision.

Stage 1

Rule-Based Eligibility Screening

Every applicant is checked against six transparent criteria: GWA, entrance exam score, household income cap, eligible course/field, year level, and location—plus any custom program requirements.

  • Import applicants individually or in bulk via CSV / Excel
  • Instant pass/fail breakdown for every single criterion
Stage 2

Priority Ranking & Shortlist

Eligible applicants are scored and ordered into a Shortlist and Waitlist based on available scholarship slots, with visual factor explanations for every score.

  • Side-by-side applicant comparison
  • Exportable CSV reports and print-ready shortlists
